# SwiftRoute Logistics Dashboard

SwiftRoute Logistics Dashboard is a comprehensive Power BI analytics solution developed to provide end-to-end visibility into logistics and supply chain operations. The project transforms operational data into actionable insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor performance, identify bottlenecks, and make informed business decisions.

The dashboard tracks critical KPIs including Total Orders, On-Time Delivery Rate, Customer Satisfaction Score (CSAT), and Average Delivery Time, with Month-over-Month (MoM) trend analysis to measure operational growth and efficiency. Interactive dashboards allow users to explore logistics performance from multiple perspectives, including Hubs, Drivers, and Vehicles.

The Hub Analytics module evaluates hub capacity utilization, order processing efficiency, and performance rankings to support workload balancing and capacity planning. The Driver Analytics section provides insights into driver ratings, experience levels, delay patterns, and delivery trends, helping identify top performers and areas for improvement. The Vehicle Analytics module focuses on fleet utilization, vehicle reliability, breakdown analysis, and order distribution across vehicle types to support maintenance and operational planning.

The solution incorporates advanced DAX calculations, data modeling techniques, KPI frameworks, drill-through functionality, and interactive filtering to deliver a dynamic user experience. Through intuitive visualizations and business-focused insights, the dashboard helps logistics teams improve service quality, optimize resource allocation, and enhance overall operational performance.
